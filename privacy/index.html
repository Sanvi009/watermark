3. How Our Tool Works

Our tool uses advanced client-side technologies to provide watermark removal functionality:

  • Local image processing: Images are processed entirely in your browser using JavaScript and Canvas APIs.
  • No data upload: Your images never leave your device or browser.
  • Temporary processing: All processing occurs in browser memory and is discarded after completion or when you close the page.

These libraries are used solely for local processing and do not transmit any data externally.
